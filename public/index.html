How a report is resolved

  1. CaptureCitizen uploads a GPS photo.
  2. PrioritizePortal scores severity.
  3. EscalateData goes to authorities.
  4. CleanGovernment team clears the site.
  5. Report backAuthority uploads cleanup proof.
